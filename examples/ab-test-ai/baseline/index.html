FAQ

Questions we get on the first call

If yours isn't here, ask it directly — we answer scoping questions before any contract exists.

Ask us anything

How quickly can we start?
Discovery sprints usually begin within two to three weeks of a signed scope. Full builds depend on squad availability; we keep a public slot count at the top of this page and we will tell you plainly if the next window is a month out.
Who owns the code and the models?
You do, unconditionally, including fine-tuned weights, prompts, eval datasets and infrastructure definitions. We keep no licence on anything delivered and we never reuse your data to improve work for another client.
Do we have to use a specific model provider?
No. We are deliberately provider-agnostic and design behind an abstraction so you can move. Where quality allows, we route cheaper traffic to smaller or open-weight models and keep frontier models for the cases that need them.
What about data residency and security?
We work in your cloud account by default, and have delivered fully on-premise and EU-only deployments. We are happy to go through your vendor security review, sign a DPA, and work under your SSO and access policies rather than ours.
What if the pilot shows it won't work?
Then the sprint did its job and we say so in writing. Roughly one in five discovery engagements ends with a recommendation not to build, usually with a cheaper non-AI alternative attached. That outcome costs you two weeks instead of two quarters.
How small a team will you work with?
We take on funded startups as well as enterprises, provided someone on your side can make decisions and give us access to data. Our smallest engagement last year was a four-person company; our largest was a bank with 9,000 staff.
