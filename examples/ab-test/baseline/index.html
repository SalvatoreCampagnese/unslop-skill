FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to try Tempo?
No. The Solo plan is free forever, and the 14-day trial of Freelance or Studio starts without any payment details. We'll email you two days before it ends.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your account drops to the free Solo plan and nothing is deleted. You can export every entry, client, and invoice as CSV or PDF at any time, including after cancelling.
Does Tempo work offline?
Yes. The desktop and mobile apps keep tracking without a connection and sync as soon as you're back online. Timers running on multiple devices are merged, not duplicated.
Can I invoice in a different currency than I track in?
You can. Set a currency per client, and Tempo converts at the rate on the invoice date — or at a fixed rate you choose, if your contract specifies one.
Will it work with my accountant's software?
Studio plans connect directly to the common accounting tools, and every plan can export invoice and time data in formats accountants accept. If yours needs something specific, email us and we'll usually add it.
Is my client data private?
Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest on EU servers. We don't run ads, don't sell data, and don't train anything on your entries. You can delete your account and all of its data from the settings page.
